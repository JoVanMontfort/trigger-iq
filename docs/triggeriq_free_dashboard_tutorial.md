# 🌿 TriggerIQ Free Dashboard Tutorial

**Theme: Customer Sentiment on Sustainability**

## 🎯 Objective

Discover how your brand’s environmental actions are perceived by customers. This tutorial guides you through the free sandbox dashboard to analyze **customer sentiment** around **sustainability topics**.

---

## ✅ Step 1: Log In and Enter the Sandbox

1. Visit [triggeriq.io](https://triggeriq.io)
2. Click **Login with Google** (or another method)
3. You’ll be taken to the free dashboard at `/dashboard/free`
   > _Note: This is a demo space using simulated data — no configuration needed._

---

## 📊 Step 2: Sentiment Overview at a Glance

The landing screen presents:

- 💬 Total sustainability-related feedback items
- 📈 Overall sentiment distribution (positive, neutral, negative)
- 🕒 Change in sentiment over the last 30 days

> _Example insight:_ “Positive sentiment rose 15% after launching your new eco-packaging.”

---

## 🌱 Step 3: Track Sentiment Over Time

- Navigate to **"Sentiment Timeline"**
- See a graph of public mood on environmental efforts
- Events like **green product launches**, **climate reports**, or **campaigns** are highlighted

> _Use case:_ Identify sentiment drops after certain campaigns to adjust messaging.

---

## 🧠 Step 4: Explore Top Environmental Topics

- Open the **"Sustainability Topics"** panel
- View keyword clusters like:
  - “carbon neutral,” “greenwashing,” “packaging,” “waste reduction”
- Keywords are colored by sentiment intensity

> _Click a topic like “greenwashing” to drill into customer concerns._

---

## 💬 Step 5: Read Actual Feedback

- Browse the **Feedback Feed** with comments pulled from:
  - Support tickets, Surveys, Social media
- Each entry shows:
  - Sentiment label
  - Highlighted sustainability term
  - Source type

> _Feedback example:_  
> “Love the idea of recycled packaging, but the box was damaged.” ➤ Sentiment: Mixed

---

## 🔍 Step 6: Filter for Deeper Analysis

Use the top bar to slice data:

- 🕓 Date range: 7, 30, or 90 days
- 📣 Source type: Email, Social, Survey
- 😊 Sentiment type: Positive, Negative, Neutral
- 🌍 Topic tags: Emissions, Biodiversity, Circular economy

> _Try filtering to “Negative + Packaging” for improvement insights._

---

## ✍️ Step 7: Try a Sentiment Test

Go to **“Try it out”** section:

- Paste or type a mock sustainability message
- Instantly see how the system analyzes sentiment and extracts themes

> _Try:_ “We planted 10,000 trees but used plastic wraps for shipping.”  
> ➤ Result: Mixed sentiment, flagged topic: Packaging
